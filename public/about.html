About Herald

A modern training and consultancy institution committed to delivering skills that matter.

Our Story

Building Skills for Better Futures

Herald Trainer and Consultant was founded with one mission: to make practical, industry-relevant education accessible to everyone — from school leavers and graduates to working professionals and business owners.

We combine classroom learning, live online sessions, and hands-on consultancy to give learners the confidence and skills they need to thrive in today's competitive environment. Every course is designed with real-world application in mind — not just theory.

Whether you're looking to upskill, reskill, or start a new career, Herald Trainer and Consultant is your trusted partner on the journey.

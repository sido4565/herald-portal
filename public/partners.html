Our Partner Institutions

Accredited awarding bodies and colleges that deliver the qualifications, lectures, and examinations for our partner programs — with Herald Trainer and Consultant providing enrollment and learner support.

How partner programs work: Herald Trainer and Consultant acts as a training and enrollment partner. Lectures are delivered by the awarding institution's faculty, and examinations are set and certified by that institution. Fees, duration, and intake dates vary by program — contact us for the latest schedule.
